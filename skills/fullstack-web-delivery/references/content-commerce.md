# Brand facts and commercial boundaries

Collect only necessary public identity, operating entity/market, services/products, contact route, content owner, assets, brand history, scope, FAQs, prices, policies, and evidence. Keep internal identification documents out of general project notes.

Create a claim/source/permission table before writing the brand story. Explain who is served, the problem, the real origin, the method, and evidence. Match the requested voice and reference qualities without copying another author's identity or protected text. Unsupported claims remain pending or become truthful process demonstrations. Fictional trial material must be labeled and kept out of real customer proof.

Portraits, recordings, video, testimonials, and customer marks require a documented purpose, public channel, term, region, editing/synthesis, withdrawal, and relevant permission. Internal processing and publicity are different uses.

For transactions identify the entity, customer type, market, product/service, currency, taxes, price basis, order, payment, delivery, subscription/renewal/cancellation, refunds, after-sales, support, and dispute process. Define server-side amounts and legal state transitions before a sandbox integration.

A requested 'final interpretation' clause should become clear interpretation, updates, conflict, and dispute rules appropriate to the actual market. Do not invent unlimited unilateral rights. Verify current authoritative sources for legal claims. Where transactions do not apply, mark the branch NOT_APPLICABLE rather than adding a fictional checkout.
