# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Website-building agents and their operators use the skill to plan and deliver a client-specific site. Developers/designers use the interaction lab to understand and verify six requested feedback patterns. The SENSU example serves fictional business visitors and isolated administrator/read-only staff.

## Product Purpose

Turn answered requirements into a complete project plan, project-specific prompt/specification documents, runnable business increments, and inspectable acceptance evidence. The interaction lab demonstrates the requested state/motion contracts without changing the existing business API.

## Operating Context

An existing English React SSR / Express / SQLite example, optional Python standard-library document generation, local browser preview, and a public KarmenLoop GitHub repository. Existing answers and authorization are reused. The current enhancement is explicitly requested; new product facts are not invented.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The specification generator and six-pattern lab are implemented under docs/upgrade-v0.5-plan.md; actual checks and remaining limits are recorded in docs/verification.md. Existing authentication, roles, inquiries and audit remain separate real business flows. Simulated lab actions must be labeled. Other hosts and physical devices require separate acceptance.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the confirmed cream/forest, restrained editorial language and existing code tokens. Public documentation remains English; agents produce client documents in the requested language. Preserve KarmenLoop copyright, the custom source-available no-modification license, independently created output exception and third-party rights.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing source, public 0.4.1 release, local/hosted automated records, actual example screenshots, and the owner's explicit six-pattern description. SENSU is fictional; there are no real customers, commercial results or human design approval claims to add.

## Product Principles

- Reuse answers and inspect technical facts.
- Make page audience, task, content, state and action explicit before implementation.
- Connect feedback to actual state; success follows the stated operation.
- Choose expression by the client's field and usage rather than a common skin.
- Keep runtime, screenshot, source, independent and device evidence distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, meaningful focus, visible state, touch alternatives and prefers-reduced-motion are required. A browser viewport does not establish physical-device acceptance.
